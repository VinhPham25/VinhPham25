### 🔬 Research Experience

**Georgia Institute of Technology** | *Undergraduate Researcher*  
**Lab:** Mind, Brain, and Education (MBE) Lab | **Advisor:** Dr. Maysam Nezafati  
**Project:** [ASTaUND](https://github.com/your-username/astaund-reproduction): Reproducible fNIRS Pipeline for Surgical Skill & Effective Connectivity Analysis  
*January 2026 – Present*

* **Objective:** Building a standardized, BIDS-compliant Python pipeline for **functional Near-Infrared Spectroscopy (fNIRS)** to compare **linear Granger Causality (GC)** and **symbolic Transfer Entropy (TE)** as measures of directed prefrontal–motor connectivity, and to develop **delay Symbolic Transfer Entropy (dSTE)** for quantifying nonlinear information flow related to surgical expertise.
* **Signal Processing:** Implemented a Homer3-aligned denoising stack—**Recursive PCA (rPCA)** motion correction and **Short-Channel Regression (OLS)**—in **MNE-Python** to isolate cortical hemodynamics from systemic noise across the ASTaUND Expert–Novice cohort.
* **Linear Connectivity:** Developed a frequency-domain **bivariate GC** pipeline (VAR-based) on ROI HbO time series as a linear effective-connectivity baseline for Expert vs Novice contrasts.
* **Symbolic TE / dSTE:** Implemented and extended **delay Symbolic Phase Transfer Entropy (dSPTE)** (Neural-Gas symbolization, multi-lag discrete TE, multivariate conditioning), then generalized the framework toward **amplitude-domain dSTE**—with continuous TE validation via **IDTxl**—as a nonlinear counterpart to GC.
* **Technical Stack:** `Python` (NumPy, SciPy, Pandas, statsmodels), `MNE-NIRS`, `IDTxl`, `YAML` Configuration Management, `BIDS`/`SNIRF` Standardization, and modular stage-based reproducibility.
